## 概述 / Overview

PTR 是东莞博可威电子科技有限公司（BKW）的金属化聚丙烯膜 AC 电容器(浸渍型)，额定温度 105℃，额定电压 160~250VAC，电容量范围 0.5μF~40μF，圆柱形外形；适用于温度补偿电路、定时与振荡回路、功率校正、开关电源耦合及 AC 滤波；如客户有特别要求，可按客户要求生产。

BKW PTR: Metallized polypropylene film AC capacitor (Dipped). Rated temperature 105℃, rated voltage 160–250VAC, capacitance 0.5μF–40μF, cylindrical body; for temperature compensation, timing and oscillator circuits, power factor correction, SMPS coupling and AC filtering. Special requirements can be produced to customer specification.

## 应用 / Applications

- 温度补偿电路 / Temperature compensation circuits
- 定时，振荡回路 / Timing, oscillator circuit
- 功率校正，开关电源耦合用 / Power factor correction and coupling capacitor in SMPS application
- AC滤波用 / AC filter capacitor
